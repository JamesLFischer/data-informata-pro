Who we are

Data Informata Pro

Data Informata Pro is a professional AI search visibility and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) measurement and reporting platform. As buyers move from ten blue links to AI-generated answers, Data Informata Pro shows brands exactly how AI assistants describe, rank and recommend them, and what it takes to be recommended more often.

Every engagement pairs measurement with action. We track where a brand appears today, identify the questions and sources where competitors are winning, and turn those findings into a prioritized plan. Monthly visual and written reports prove the progress, on the same locked questions every month.

Plain-English guide

What do these terms mean?

Share of Model (SoM)
How often AI assistants mention and recommend your business when people ask them questions, shown as a percentage. Higher is better. Think of it as your share of the conversation inside AI answers, scored higher when you're named first.
Share of Voice
Out of all the businesses the AI names across every answer, the slice that is yours. If the AI names 10 businesses in total and 4 of them are you, your share of voice is 40%.
AI engine
An AI assistant people ask questions to. We check ChatGPT (by OpenAI), Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini (by Google) and Claude (by Anthropic).
Google AI Overviews
The AI-written summary that now appears at the top of many Google search results, above the usual list of websites.
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
Work that helps AI assistants know, trust and recommend a business. It's like SEO, but aimed at AI answers instead of the list of Google links.
Prompt / question
A question a real customer might type into an AI assistant, for example “Who is the best injury lawyer near me?”
Locked prompt set
The fixed list of questions we ask every month. Keeping it the same makes month-to-month comparisons fair.
Unbranded question
A question that doesn't name your business, like “What's the best plumber in Seattle?”. It shows whether new customers discover you.
Branded question
A question that names your business, like “Is Smith Plumbing any good?”. It shows what the AI says about your reputation.
Mention rate / inclusion rate
The percentage of answers that include your business at all.
Discovery vs. recognition
Discovery is how often you show up when people don't ask for you by name. Recognition is how often the AI knows you when they do.
#1 recommendation
The answer names your business first, the top pick.
Top 3
Your business is named among the first three options in the answer.
Sentiment
Whether the AI talks about you positively, neutrally or negatively. It's shown as a score from 0 to 100, where 50 is neutral and higher is more positive.
Citation / cited source
A website the AI points to as where it got its information, like a footnote. When the AI cites your own website, that's a strong sign of trust.
Own-site citations
How many answers listed your own website as a source.
Source mix
The kinds of websites the AI relies on: your site, review and directory sites, news and industry sites, or competitors' sites.
Competitors
The other businesses we compare you against. “Provisional” means the list hasn't been confirmed with you yet.
Who appears instead
Competitors the AI named in answers where your business was missing.
Opportunity
A question where your business isn't showing up yet. These are the places to improve next.
Buying intent
How close the person asking is to making a purchase. “Best plumber near me” is high intent; “What does a plumber do?” is early research.
Topic cluster
A group of related questions, like “Reputation” or “Comparisons”.
Heatmap
A color grid: greener means you show up more often, orange means less.
Run / measurement
One complete round of asking every question to every AI engine and recording the answers.
Baseline
The first measurement, used as the starting point to show progress over time.
Month over month (MoM)
The change since the previous measurement. ▲ means up and ▼ means down.
Percentage points (pts)
The simple difference between two percentages. Going from 40% to 46% is +6 points.
Citation fidelity
How accurately the AI states your facts (location, services, products).
Specialized AI agent
Our assistant inside the dashboard. It answers data questions instantly and passes strategy questions to a specialist analyst.
